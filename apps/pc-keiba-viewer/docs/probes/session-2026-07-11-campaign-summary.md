## Methodology notes this session reinforced or introduced

- **Noise floor ±0.4pp**: pure-seed-retraining variance on identical features/folds measures pooled top1 delta +0.15–+0.31pp (up to +0.4053pp at the extreme draw) — any single-arm claim inside this band is not distinguishable from retraining noise (`tmp/dead-lever-retest/seed_noise_floor.json`).
- **Cell-level adoption directive**: mid-session directive that pooled numbers alone are forbidden as an adoption basis — every architecture/feature test now requires a per-cell/per-tier blind-fold scan before REJECT is final (applied retroactively to volatility-tiered fusion).
- **Pre-registered-hypothesis-only adoption**: the 956-cell Bonferroni-corrected sweep (`tmp/rc2-cell-atlas/variant_opportunity_report.json`) found 18 nominal passes but 0 survive correction — even the already-deployed, already-proven rule (`class_label=703`) would not have survived a blind sweep. Conclusion: new cell-routing rules must come from targeted, substantively-motivated, independently blind-verified single hypotheses (the pattern that produced the real deploys), never from mining a blind multi-cell sweep.
- **I²-discipline**: national variance decomposition across venue/surface/distance cross-cuts (`tmp/rc2-cell-atlas/national_variance_decomp.json`) shows I² in the 0-50% range almost everywhere (1 of 20 level×metric tests nominally significant, exactly the false-positive rate expected by chance, 0 survive Bonferroni) — reinforces `feedback_harness_sort_before_mask` and confirms there is no large hidden cell-level heterogeneity left to mine nationally.
